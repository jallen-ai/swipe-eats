Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

SwipeEats is a restaurant discovery app that helps you and your friends decide where to eat. We take your privacy seriously and collect only what's necessary to make the app work.

What We Collect

  • Anonymous ID: When you open SwipeEats, we create an anonymous session identifier. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or any login credentials.
  • Location: With your permission, we use your device's location to find restaurants near you. Your location is sent to our server to query nearby restaurants and is not stored beyond the current session.
  • Swipe Data: Your restaurant swipe choices (right/left) are stored temporarily for the duration of your session to calculate matches, especially in group mode.
  • Group Session Data: When you create or join a group session, we store a session code, group name, member nicknames, and swipe data to coordinate the group experience. This data expires after 2 hours.

What We Don't Collect

  • Names, email addresses, or passwords
  • Payment or financial information
  • Browsing history or activity outside SwipeEats
  • Contacts, photos, or other device data

Data Retention

  • Session data (swipes, matches, group info) expires automatically after 2 hours.
  • Restaurant data is cached for up to 30 days per Google's API requirements, then refreshed.
  • No personal data is retained long-term.

Third-Party Services

  • Google Places API: We use Google's Places API to find restaurants, photos, and details near you. Google's privacy policy applies to data processed by their API. Google Privacy Policy
  • Supabase: Our backend infrastructure (database, authentication, file storage) is hosted on Supabase. Supabase Privacy Policy

Cookies & Tracking

SwipeEats does not use cookies or third-party tracking scripts. We store a minimal authentication token in your browser's local storage to maintain your anonymous session.

Children's Privacy

SwipeEats is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach out at privacy@swipeeats.app
